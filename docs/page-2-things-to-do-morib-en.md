Morib is a quiet seaside town in Banting, Kuala Langat, about an hour from Kuala Lumpur. Staying at IndahMorib puts you minutes from the beach, and the heritage and day-trip spots below are a short drive away. Drive times are estimates until we check them on Google Maps.

2. **Dataran Pantai Morib** (promenade, playgrounds, food stalls): same stretch as above.

## Pantai Morib: what to expect

### Is Morib Beach good for swimming?

No. The water is shallow and muddy, so swimming is not recommended. Come for walks, kite flying and sunset. The beach is muddy at low tide.

### Sunset and best time to visit

Sunset falls around 6:30 to 7:30 pm.

### Facilities

The promenade has gazebos, playgrounds, gasing (top-spinning) arenas, a stage, and food stalls. Public toilets, showers, parking, and a prayer room are available. Entry is free.

### The 1945 landing

On 9 September 1945, Allied forces landed at Morib, part of Operation Zipper. Some records also cite Operation Tiderace. A memorial slab stands in the turning circle at Morib Beach. 

## Bukit Jugra and the Jugra heritage route

### Bukit Jugra hike

The hill is 146 m high. The trail is easy and takes about 1 to 1.5 hours to reach the top. Two lighthouses stand at the summit: a colonial-era one and a 25 m concrete tower built in 1976. Views show the Langat River meeting the sea.

### Paragliding

Tandem paragliding takes off from the base of the lighthouse. Opening hours differ between listings. 

### Istana Bandar (Istana Alaeddin)

The palace was built in the early 1900s.  It is open to the public and is empty inside. Beside it is Masjid Bandar.

### Jugra Royal Mausoleum and museum

The royal mausoleum has been the burial site of the Selangor royal family since the 1880s. Museum Insitu Jugra occupies a restored 1875 police station.

## Pantai Kelanang and the mangroves

A 3 km beach fringed by mangroves, about 6 to 8 km from Morib. It is a quieter option.

## Tanjung Sepat and Pulau Carey day trips

**Tanjung Sepat** is a fishing village with boats moored at low tide. Lover's Bridge and seafood stalls are nearby. Pair it with a seafood lunch.

**Pulau Carey, Mah Meri Cultural Village** shows Mah Meri wood carving and culture. Entry is quoted from RM5 by. Weekday visits are mostly by prior booking. Call before you go: +603 2282 3135 / +603 2161 0577.

## FAQ

**Is Morib Beach good for swimming?**
No. The water is shallow and muddy. Come for walks, kites and sunsets.

**Is Pantai Morib free to enter?**
Entry is free. 

**How far is Morib from Kuala Lumpur?**  
 65 to 80 km, and drive times of about 1 to 1.5 hours.

**What happened at Morib in 1945?**
The 9 September 1945 Allied landing at Morib, part of Operation Zipper. Some records also cite Operation Tiderace.

**How long does the Bukit Jugra hike take?**
About 1 to 1.5 hours to the lighthouse, rated easy.

**What can I see in Jugra?**
Istana Bandar, the Jugra Royal Mausoleum, Museum Insitu Jugra and Masjid Bandar.

**What is there to do in Morib besides the beach?**
Bukit Jugra, the Jugra heritage route, Tanjung Sepat, Pulau Carey and Pantai Kelanang.
