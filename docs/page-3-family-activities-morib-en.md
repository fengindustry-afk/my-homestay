## Free activities at Pantai Morib

### Playgrounds and open space

The promenade has children's playgrounds, gazebos and a stage. Gasing (traditional top-spinning) arenas are on site. [VERIFY current condition.]

### Kite flying

Kite flying is the most popular beach activity. Bring a kite, or buy or rent one from vendors near the beach [VERIFY vendors].

### Shell-hunting at low tide

Shell-hunting is possible at low tide. The beach is muddy at low tide, so bring shoes and supervise children closely.

### Safety

Swimming is not recommended. The water is shallow and muddy. Keep young children within reach of an adult at all times.
