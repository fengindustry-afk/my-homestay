### Golden Sea Restaurant Morib

Covered seating beside the beach. Reviewers praise kam heong baby squid and steamed grouper. Go before sunset if you want the beach view [VERIFY]. Halal status: please confirm with the restaurant. Service and freshness reports vary, so check your order when it arrives.
